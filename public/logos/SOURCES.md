These assets were downloaded from public logo collections and saved locally. The page never requests a third-party logo service at runtime. Original asset files are retained without changes.

The Simple Icons and gilbarbara/logos collections distribute their assets under CC0. Brand marks belong to their respective owners and identify the organizations mentioned in the résumé.

Logos for Blend, CVS Health, Even & Odd Minds, Thomson Reuters, Brooks Brothers, and Consumer Reports are pending access to their online sources. Their company names and links remain available. Add a downloaded asset under this directory and its relative path in `content/resume.json` to display another logo.
